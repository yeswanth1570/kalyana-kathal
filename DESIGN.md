# Kalyana Kathal: The Life Quest

## Game design: first playable slice

**Format:** Single-player 2D action platformer with a character-driven dialogue encounter  
**Engine:** Godot 4  
**Slice:** College corridor, focused on the reference image's Sharmila scene

### The idea

Murugan is trying to make a sincere first impression on Sharmila during a hectic college day. The player weaves through the corridor, jumps over flying campus paperwork, and tosses harmless folded love letters to open a short dialogue moment. The encounter rewards respectful, specific replies and lets pushy behavior backfire.

### Visual direction

A warm, hand-inked storybook look: aged cream paper, sepia hallway perspective, deep maroon outlines, muted school-uniform colors, and small rose-red accents for affection and danger. UI panels resemble vintage Tamil cinema title cards. Characters use expressive, readable poses and slightly exaggerated silhouettes. The supplied image is a mood and layout reference; the prototype draws original placeholder characters and does not reproduce its generated artwork.

### Play loop

1. Move across the corridor and jump over incoming paper hazards.
2. Throw love letters to lower Sharmila's guardedness and trigger a conversation.
3. Choose a response: considerate and specific replies raise interest; pressure lowers it.
4. Reach a positive outcome to clear the encounter.

### Controls

| Action | Keyboard |
| --- | --- |
| Move | A / D or arrow keys |
| Jump | Space / W |
| Dash | Shift |
| Throw letter | J / X |
| Talk | R |
| Dialogue choice | 1, 2, or 3 |
| Retry | Enter |

### Slice success criteria

- Movement, jumping, dash, projectile, hazards, health, and interest are all readable in play.
- Dialogue pauses action and offers three distinct player choices.
- The encounter has success, setback, and retry feedback.
- The slice works offline and does not capture or upload player audio.

### Scope beyond this slice

The supplied design brief outlines five life stages: school corridor, college campus, IT office, family introduction, and wedding mandapam. Those are the intended campaign direction, not content in this first prototype. Voice input, generated dialogue, and training-data collection are also out of scope; any future voice feature should be opt-in, clearly explained, and designed with data minimization.

### Originality note

The project takes broad inspiration from challenging 2D boss encounters and 1930s-inspired cartoon presentation. It uses original characters, Tamil college-romance themes, hand-drawn placeholder visuals, and its own dialogue and setting rather than copying Cuphead's characters, art, music, or levels.
